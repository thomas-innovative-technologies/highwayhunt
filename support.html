Common questions

How is the card built for my route? Pick the card for where you're driving (USA, India or Europe), then where you're starting and where you're headed. Plates from states or countries along the way are common; far-off ones (and islands like Hawaii, Lakshadweep or Iceland) are rare. Your home state or country is the FREE square.

How do I spot a European country? Look for the letters on the blue band at the left of the plate (D for Germany, F for France, NL for the Netherlands), or on an oval sticker. Each square's hint tells you what to look for.

What's free? Your first trip: every card for 3 days from your first game. After that the Trip Pass ($2.99, one time) unlocks every card for good, plus Linked Play and every new card and region we add. Family Sharing covers your family.

How do I play with friends on other phones? Each phone starts its own game. Pick one game code and have everyone type it on the New game screen, with the same card, trip and difficulty. Everyone still gets their own card.

How do I check someone's Bingo? Start a game with their code, their name spelled the same way, and the same card, trip and difficulty. You'll see their exact card.

What is Same card? Everyone who picks Same card that day gets the identical card on any phone, so you can race another car.

How does Linked Play work? With the Trip Pass, phones playing the same card and code link up by themselves: nearby over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi (iPhone asks once for Local Network permission), and across cars when there's a signal. Everyone sees each other's finds, and every phone celebrates and checks a Bingo. You can turn it off in Settings.

I bought the Trip Pass on another device. Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Can I delete my data? Everything is stored only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it all, including photos you took in the app.
