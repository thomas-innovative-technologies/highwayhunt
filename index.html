HIGHWAY HUNT

Road Trip Bingo

Turn the drive into a game. Tell Highway Hunt where you're starting and where you're headed, and everyone in the car gets their own bingo card built for your route: license plates from the states or countries along the way, big rigs, RVs, road signs and more. Plates from far-off states are rare and glow red. First to complete the pattern calls BINGO!

  • Road trips in the USA, India and Europe, plus a Beach Trip card and a 3×3 Kids card for little ones
  • Pass one phone around, or link phones live in the same car or across cars
  • Family leaderboard and badges, like spotting plates from all 50 states or 10 European countries
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Your first trip is free.

Passengers play. The driver drives.

For iPhone and iPad.
